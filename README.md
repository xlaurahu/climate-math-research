# Spatial Estimation of Mean Surface Air Temperature in San Diego County

We used elevation and climatology between 1976 and 2025 to estimate the regional mean temperature of San Diego County. Statistical, physical, as well as supervised machine learning methods are used to interpolate the mean temperature. The methods provide high-resolution regional mean temperature based on the county elevation data. 



## Data used are stored [HERE](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1kLp1J4CJ-sAnq76FLtKVFiW3ADvY-STt)

> [!NOTE]
>In the IPython notebook file, Regression Kriging was interpolated on a 50 × 50 grid in order to achieve faster computation. Users may increase the grid resolution to 100 × 100 to produce a higher-resolution interpolation product.
